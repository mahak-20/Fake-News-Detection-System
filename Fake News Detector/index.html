About this tool: This is an educational demo of a Multinomial Naive Bayes text classifier, trained entirely client-side on a small, hand-written corpus of about 80 example headlines. It picks up on surface patterns — sensational wording, hedged attribution, punctuation — not verified facts. It will misjudge real headlines that use urgent language and fake ones written in a measured tone, and it has no knowledge of current events. Treat the verdict as a demonstration of how the algorithm works, not a fact-check.
